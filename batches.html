01The week

Eight accounts. On any given day four hold a position and four are flat. The pair sharing a day takes the identical trade.

Four accounts, two outcomes

A1 and A2 take the same signal at the same moment, so they pass or fail together. A batch of four is two distinct attempts held twice — two fees per attempt. Starting the second account of each pair a week later would give four different outcomes at the same speed.

02How much room 2.5% leaves

Risk is fixed at 2.5% per trade. The question is not what the maximum could be, it is whether 2.5% keeps a single bad trade clear of the 3% daily limit — because one trade past it ends the account on its own, whatever the running total says.

At 2.5% per trade, a loss of 1.20 R is the whole daily limit. Headroom is what is left between the worst loss actually observed and that.

The A batch has little room, and it is measured room

The opening range fills past its stop on 24 of 75 trades — real-tick execution, spread and slippage included — with a worst case of -1.094 R. At 2.5% that costs 2.74% of the 3.00% daily limit, leaving 0.26%. A fill only 10% worse than the worst one on record would breach the day on a single trade.

The B batch's larger headroom is not real

Every fade stop fills at exactly −1.000 R, because that study replays M1 bars and takes the stop at its level — it models no slippage past the stop at all. Its 0.50% of apparent headroom is therefore optimistic. Give it the same overshoot the A batch actually shows and it lands where the A batch does.

04What rotation costs

An account on two weekdays sees about half the signals, so it reaches +12% later and more attempts run out of deadline.

What the rotation is actually buying

It is not throughput — each account is slower. It is that the two pairs hold different days, so no single bad session can take the whole batch. All four fail together only 1.4% of the time in batch A and 8.6% in batch B.

Rotate the assignment, not just the accounts

Because the weekdays are fixed, any real day-of-week effect would land on the same pair for ever. Swapping which pair takes which days each week costs nothing and removes that exposure.
