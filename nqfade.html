02The rules

Draw one fib on the range before the session. Everything else reads off it.

Step
The rangeThe high and low of 00:00–13:30 UTC
Draw the fibAnchor 0 on the level you are fading, 1 on the other side. Longs: drag low→high. Shorts: high→low.
The sweepPrice trades beyond the level during 13:30–20:00 UTC, the New York session
The entryAn M5 candle closes back inside the level. Enter at that close, at market. One trade a day — the first signal only.
The stopThe −0.25 fib — 25% of the range beyond the level
The targetThe 0.50 fib — 50% of the range back inside. Reward:risk therefore varies; median 1.24.
The stop moveNone. Moving it to breakeven or to −0.5R was measured on both strategies and lost money every way it was tried.
The exitStop, target, or the close of the window — whichever comes first
DaysMonday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Wednesday is excluded — the only losing day of the week, negative in six months of nine, and in all three years tested.

05Market or limit?

The signal is the same either way. This is only about the fill.

The confirming candle closes some way back inside the level, and you buy that close. The alternative is to rest a limit between the level and that close and wait for price to come back: a better price when it fills, and no trade at all when it does not. Because the stop and the target are both pinned to the level, a fill at the level is always 0.50 / 0.25 — the reward to risk stops varying.

07What this does not show

  • 2026 only. 117 trades on 143 eligible days. Tested on 2024 and 2025 as well, which these parameters were not chosen on: positive in both (+0.047 and +0.053 R a trade against +0.246 here), 24 of 33 months green across the three years, and Wednesday negative in every one. The out-of-sample expectancy is +0.050 R, a fifth of what this page shows, and the worst losing run is 9 rather than 4. Take the 2026 figures below as the best case, not the expectation.
  • Bar replay, not the Strategy Tester. Entries and exits are walked over M1 bars with a fixed 1.5-point spread and commission taken off the R. A short's stop is checked against the ask.
  • Intrabar order is unknown. An M1 bar does not say whether its high or low came first, which can move an exit by a minute on a handful of charts.
  • The parameters were chosen on this data. The stop and target levels came out of a sweep over this same year, so the exact figures are optimistic even where the shape of the result is not.
