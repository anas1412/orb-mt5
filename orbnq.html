The worst single day across both was -2.09 R — -5.24% at 2.5% risk. A 3% daily limit is breached by two full losses at 2.5% risk, so position size has to be set for the number of strategies running, not for one of them.

06What this does not show

  • 2026 only. The NQ fade has since been run on 2024 and 2025 as well and stays positive, at about a fifth of the expectancy it shows here. Nothing else on this page has an out-of-sample test behind it.
  • The ORB is real-tick Strategy Tester output; the fades are bar replays with an assumed spread. They are not measured to the same standard and the fades will read slightly optimistic.
  • The fade parameters were chosen on this data. Stop and target levels came out of sweeps over this same year.
  • Pooling assumes equal risk on every trade and ignores that simultaneous losses breach a 3% daily limit long before the drawdown figure does.
