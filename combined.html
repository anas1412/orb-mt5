The drawdowns do not add up

On the 28 days both strategies fired, their results are negatively correlated at -0.35 — when one lost the other tended to win. Both lost on the same day 2 times in the year. One is a breakout and the other fades a failed breakout, so that they do not fail together is structural rather than lucky, but 28 days is a small sample to measure a correlation on.

What the limits allow

A 12% maximum loss is reached by the worst run at 1.81% risk per trade and by the worst drawdown at 1.81%. A 3% daily limit is breached by the worst day at 1.97%. The binding constraint is the day, because two strategies can lose on it, and that is what caps this book below either strategy traded alone.
